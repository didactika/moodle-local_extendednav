---
title: Administrators see every node
summary: Why a site administrator sees the nodes that are hidden or restricted, and how to apply the rules to them too.
order: 40
---

By default, a **site administrator sees every node** of every navigation, even one that is hidden or restricted to roles the administrator does not have. No rule can leave a site without its administrator, and an administrator can always check the navigation that other people will see.

When an administrator sees a node only for being one, the column **Visibility** of the management page shows **Only admins** beside its visibility, so it is clear that other people do not see it.

## Apply the rules to administrators too

Turn on **Hide from administrators too** when you hide nodes (the question asked before hiding offers it), or in the form of the node, under **Who sees it**. The node then follows its rules for administrators as well. Each node decides it by itself. In the site administration it is on by default.

To see what other people see, log in as a user with the role you want to check.

## What does not change

- The language of a node still applies to everyone: it is not a permission.
- **Site administration** in the primary navigation cannot be hidden from anyone who has access to it, and the way to the management page of the plugin is never hidden in the site administration.
- Blocking the address of a hidden node never blocks an administrator, unless the node is hidden from administrators too.
