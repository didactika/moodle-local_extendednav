title: Change a navigation for a category or a course
summary: Give a category or a course its own order, names and nodes, while the rest of the site keeps the navigation of the site.

The navigation of a course, of an activity and of a category can be changed for the whole site and, as an exception, for a **category** or a **course**. Everything inside a category follows it, and a course can change what its category sets.

## Choose the scope

On the management page, open **Applies to** and search for the category or the course. The menu also lists the ones that already have changes, with how many. Choose **Whole site** to go back.

<Screenshot src="asset:manage-scope-menu.webp" alt="The menu that chooses the scope, with a search and the courses found" caption="Search a category or a course to change its navigation." url="/local/extendednav/manage.php?menu=course" />

## Make an exception

With a category or a course chosen, the list shows what its pages show: the nodes of the site and the ones it inherits. Change a node as you would for the site: hide it, move it or edit it. The first change of a node gives the category or the course a row of its own, which starts as a copy of the node it replaces. The row shows **Exception**; a node that comes from a category around it shows **Inherited**.

<Screenshot src="asset:manage-exception.webp" alt="The navigation of a course, with a node marked as an exception" caption="A node changed only for this course." url="/local/extendednav/manage.php?menu=course" />

- The nearest scope wins: the course over its category, and the category over the site.
- When a category or a course changes the order of any node, it sets the order of its whole navigation.
- A node added for a course exists only there.

## Go back to the site

The button at the end of the row of an exception (**Go back**) removes the changes of the category or the course for that node, and its navigation follows the site again. Deleting a node of the site removes its exceptions too.

## Export and import

The exceptions of a **course** are part of the course: they go in its backup and come back when it is restored, also as a new course or into another one, with the groups, the groupings and the dates of their conditions matched to the course restored. A node that the course already changes is kept as it is.

The exceptions of a **category** belong to the site, as Moodle does not back up categories: **Export > This navigation** and **Export > Every navigation** include them, each with its category (`scope`). On import, the category is looked up by its full name, with its parents. See [The YAML format](page:reference/yaml-format).
