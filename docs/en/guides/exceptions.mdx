title: Change a navigation for a category or a course
summary: Give a category or a course its own order, names and nodes, while the rest of the site keeps the navigation of the site.

---

The navigation of a course, of an activity and of a category can be changed for the whole site and, as an exception, for a **category** or a **course**. Everything inside a category follows it, and a course can change what its category sets.

## Open the navigation of a category or a course

In a course, open the menu of the course (**More**) and select **Navigation**. In a category, open the menu of the category and select **Navigation**. The page lists the navigations that the category or the course can change: a course changes its own navigation and the one of its activities; a category also changes its own and the ones of the courses inside it.

## Who can do it

Whoever manages the navigation of the site can change the navigation of every category and course. Whoever has the capability **Manage the navigation of a category or a course** (`local/extendednav:managecontext`) in a category or a course can change that one and everything inside it. Managers have it by default; give it to other roles, such as teachers, in a course or a category with the permissions of Moodle.

## See every exception

In **Site administration > Appearance > Extended navigation > Exceptions of categories and courses**, the list shows every category and course that changes a navigation, with the number of nodes it changes in each one. Search by name, narrow the list to categories or to courses, and select one to open its page.

<Screenshot src="asset:exceptions-overview.webp" alt="The list of the categories and courses that change a navigation" caption="Every exception of the site, in one list." url="/local/extendednav/exceptions.php" />

## Make an exception

The list shows what the pages of the category or the course show: the nodes of the site and the ones it inherits. Change a node as you would for the site: hide it, move it or edit it. The first change of a node gives the category or the course a row of its own, which starts as a copy of the node it replaces. The row shows **Exception**; a node that comes from a category around it shows **Inherited**.

- The nearest scope wins: the course over its category, and the category over the site.
- When a category or a course changes the order of any node, it sets the order of its whole navigation.
- A node added for a course exists only there.

## Go back to the site

The button at the end of the row of an exception (**Go back**) removes the changes of the category or the course for that node, and its navigation follows the site again. Deleting a node of the site removes its exceptions too.

## Export and import

The exceptions of a **course** are part of the course: they go in its backup and come back when it is restored, also as a new course or into another one, with the groups, the groupings and the dates of their conditions matched to the course restored. A node that the course already changes is kept as it is.

The exceptions of a **category** belong to the site, as Moodle does not back up categories: **Export > This navigation** and **Export > Every navigation** include them, each with its category (`scope`). On import, the category is looked up by its full name, with its parents. See [The YAML format](page:reference/yaml-format).
