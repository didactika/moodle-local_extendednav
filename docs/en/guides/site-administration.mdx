The site administration is a tree: categories such as **Users** or **Plugins** hold pages and other categories. The plugin reads that tree on every administration page, so every category and every page can be changed, however deep it is.

<Screenshot src="asset:manage-admin-top.webp" alt="The site administration on the management page, with its top-level categories" caption="The top level of the site administration." url="/local/extendednav/manage.php?menu=admin" />

## Browse the tree

Choose **Site administration** in the column of the management page. The list shows one level of the tree. The top level has the categories that Moodle shows as tabs (**Users**, **Courses**, **Plugins**…) and, first, **General**, which holds the other items of the top level, as the tab General of Moodle does. A category has a small folder after its name; select its name to open it. The path above the list shows where you are, from **Site administration**; select a level to go back to it. A search looks in every level at once.

<Screenshot src="asset:manage-admin-level.webp" alt="The category Users opened, with its categories and the path above the list" caption="A category opened, with the way back in the path." url="/local/extendednav/manage.php?menu=admin&parent=users" />

## What you can do

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Rename a category or a page | Edit it and fill **Title**. The new name is shown in the administration, in the breadcrumb and in the search |
| Hide it | The eye button of its row, or restrict it to some roles or languages in its form |
| Order it | Drag it by its handle among the others of its category |
| Add a page of your own | **Add node** with the **Type** **Page**, a title, a URL and the **Parent**: a tab, a category or **General**, where Moodle shows the pages of the top level |
| Add a category of your own | **Add node** with the **Type** **Category**, a title and the **Parent**, the tab or the category it goes in. It has no URL |
| Add a tab of your own | **Add node** with the **Type** **Tab** and a title. It has no parent or URL, and goes at the top |

The **Parent** depends on the **Type**: a tab has none, a category goes in a tab or in another category, and a page goes in a tab, a category or **General**. After you save a tab or a category, the list opens inside it, to add its pages. Moodle shows a tab or a category only once it holds a page, so while it is empty the list marks it **Empty, not shown**; it stays listed at the top, so you can open it later and add its pages.

A tab that holds pages and no category shows them in a section of its own, with the name of the tab, so it is a tab as Moodle's are. Moodle puts the tabs in the row in the order of the list and sends the ones that do not fit to **More**: drag a tab up to have it in the row.

The URL of a page of Moodle cannot change, and its settings stay where they are: renaming or hiding a page changes how it is listed, never what it does. Moodle still checks the capabilities of each page.

## Pages that Moodle hides

Some pages are hidden by Moodle itself, such as the ones it opens after an upgrade. They are listed with **Hidden by Moodle** under their visibility, and the plugin does not show them.

## Administrators

In the site administration, **Hide from administrators too** is on by default: a category or a page that you hide is hidden from the administrators as well. The question asked when you hide nodes lets you turn it off, and so does the form of each node. The way to this management page is never hidden. See [Administrators see every node](page:concepts/administrators-see-everything).
