---
title: Order the items
summary: Drag an item by its handle to its new place, or move it with the arrow keys.
order: 40
---

Drag an item by the handle at the start of its row and drop it where it goes. A line under or over a row shows where it will land. The order is saved as soon as you drop it, and the list is drawn again without reloading the page.

<Screenshot src="asset:manage-primary.webp" alt="The management page, with a handle at the start of each row" caption="Each row has its handle." url="/local/extendednav/manage.php" />

With the keyboard, move the focus to the handle of an item and press the **Up arrow** or the **Down arrow**: the item moves one place.

- An item moves among its siblings: the items with its parent, or the categories and pages of its category in the site administration.
- Select several nodes and drag one of them: the nodes selected among its siblings move together, in the order they have.
- Moving a parent moves its submenu items with it.
- The handles are not shown while a search or a filter is applied, because the list then shows only a part of the navigation. Use the button that clears the filters to order again.
- On a narrow screen, such as a phone, the handles are not shown: order the navigation from a wider screen.
- In a category or a course, ordering an item gives it the order of its own navigation. See [Change a navigation for a category or a course](page:guides/exceptions).

The order of the list is the order of the navigation, and an exported file keeps it: every item has its position in the field `order`.
