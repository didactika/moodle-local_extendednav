---
title: Order the items
summary: Change the position of the items with the arrow buttons of their row.
order: 40
---

Move an item with the **Move up** and **Move down** buttons of its row. They are buttons, so they work with the keyboard and a screen reader, and the list is saved and drawn again at once.

<Screenshot src="asset:manage-primary.webp" alt="The management page, with the arrow buttons at the end of each row" caption="Each row has its arrow buttons." url="/local/extendednav/manage.php" />

- Moving a parent moves its submenu items with it.
- A submenu item only moves among its siblings.
- An item that leaves its parent stays right below the group of that parent.
- The arrows are off while a search or a filter is applied. Use the button that clears the filters to order again.
- The navigations of a course, an activity and a category are ordered the same way, for the whole site or for one category or course.

The order of the list is the order of the menu, and an exported file keeps it: every item has its position in the field `order`.
